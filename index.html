SurveySafe

Stop finding problems at the wall

One missed condition on the site survey — wall out of plumb, floor running away, skirting clash, carpet too thick, no access — and a profitable job turns into a return visit, a remake, or an on-site bodge you find out about three weeks later. SurveySafe walks you through every check so nothing gets missed.

How it works

Four steps. No typing.

1

Pick sliding or hinged

Choose once at the start. It switches the relevant checks on or off — you only see questions that apply to your job.

2

Walk the room, tap the band

Each check has coloured bands (e.g. wall plumb: under 10 mm / 10–20 mm / over 20 mm). Tap the one that applies. The tool knows the flag colour from the band — no typing unless you want to record an exact reading.

3

Clear low-risk sections fast

Nothing to flag? Hit “Mark all as fine” and move on. It’s per section, so you still eyeball each area.

4

Get a report the whole team can read

A clean PDF survey plus a red/amber/green risk summary: every red and amber listed in plain English alongside what to do about it. Weeks later, anyone can pick it up.
